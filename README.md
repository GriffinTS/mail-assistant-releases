# Mail Assistant: downloads

Mail Assistant is a menu bar app that organizes Apple Mail using Apple Intelligence on your Mac. It suggests
to-dos and calendar events from your email, files newsletters and receipts, and moves junk. Your email never
leaves your Mac.

## ⬇️ [Download the latest version](https://github.com/GriffinTS/mail-assistant-releases/releases/latest)

### Installing
1. On the download page, under **Assets**, click **MailAssistant-x.y.z.zip**.
2. Open your **Downloads** folder and double-click the zip. An app called **Mail Assistant** appears.
3. Drag **Mail Assistant** onto **Applications** in the Finder sidebar.
4. Open **Applications** and double-click **Mail Assistant**.
   If macOS says it can't verify the app, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**,
   scroll down, click **Open Anyway**, and enter your Mac password.
5. The **Welcome** window asks which inboxes to organize and how far back to look. Choose, then press **Start**.
6. When macOS asks, allow Mail Assistant to control **Mail** and to use **Reminders** and **Calendars**.

You'll need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on
(System Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri).

### Updating
Nothing to download. Mail Assistant shows **Update available** and installs it with one click.
If that ever fails, open **Settings → Step-by-Step Update Instructions…** in the app.
